# Third-Party Design Materials

This public portfolio uses the following presentation-only resources. The demos keep all business data and interactions local.

- **Noto Sans SC**: loaded from Google Fonts. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **DM Mono**: loaded from Google Fonts. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Lucide**: `lucide-react` is bundled for the feedback Agent; the financial evaluation workbench loads the matching fixed `0.468.0` UMD build from unpkg. Licensed under the ISC License.

The workbench's CDN resources have system-font and text-content fallbacks, so failed font or icon requests do not affect the local demo flows.
